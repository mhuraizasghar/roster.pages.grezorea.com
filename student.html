Student Guide & Rules

  1. Sign In: Enter your name and enrolled university email.
  2. Select Quiz: Review the time limit, passing percentage, and allowed attempts.
  3. Question Navigator: Numbers at the top indicate your progress. Answered questions turn blue.
  4. Auto-Submit: When the countdown timer reaches 00:00, the quiz automatically submits.
  5. Instant Review: Inspect complete question explanations and your leaderboard rank.
Your submissions and individual answer selections remain strictly private to your enrolled email and course instructor.
